# CloudDesk — fictional SaaS knowledge v1

This is the committed example dataset. All facts below are mock data for this
learning project, not policies, prices, or commitments of a real business.
Copy this file to `knowledge/mock-saas.md` for local customization, then restart
the server after edits. Keep credentials and customer records out.

## Product

CloudDesk is a fictional web app for small teams to organize shared tasks and
project boards. It supports task assignments, due dates, comments, and CSV exports.
It does not offer a native mobile app in this demo.

## Plans and billing

- Starter: USD 12 per workspace per month, up to 5 members and 3 project boards.
- Team: USD 29 per workspace per month, up to 20 members and unlimited project boards.
- New workspaces have a 14-day trial. A payment card is not required for the trial.
- Paid plans are billed monthly in USD. Annual billing is not offered in this demo.
- A workspace owner can view invoices in Settings → Billing → Invoices.

## Getting started

Create a workspace, create a project board, and invite teammates through
Settings → Members. Only workspace owners can manage billing and member invites.

## Cancellation and support

- The workspace owner can request cancellation in Settings → Billing → Cancel plan.
- Under this fictional policy, cancellation stops the next renewal and access
  continues until the end of the current paid billing period.
- The chatbot cannot perform or verify a cancellation.
- Human support is available through Help → Contact support inside CloudDesk.
- Support hours: Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 Asia/Jakarta. No response-time guarantee is supplied.

## Intentionally unknown

No refund policy, enterprise pricing, uptime SLA, data-residency commitment,
or customer-specific account/order/payment records are supplied.
Questions about these must acknowledge missing information.
